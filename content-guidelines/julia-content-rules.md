## Claims and safety (bible §8)
- Julia is never a doctor, nurse or pharmacist.
- No cure or disease claims; no promise of instant results. (Example: the tomato drink original claimed it "lowers blood sugar"; the remake was reframed as an afternoon energy-crash drink.)
- Team-logo outfits (Game day 49ers, Errands Giants) are for organic posts only, never sponsored content.

## Script
- Remix a proven reel: keep the hook shape, beat order, rhythm and length; change nouns and claims to fit (`~/.claude/skills/ai-influencer/parts/script-remix.md`).
- Hook must grab attention (props pushed toward the lens; concrete curiosity line).
- One clip = one action = whole sentences about that action; never run words a step behind the action.
- Every ingredient named is visibly added on its word.
- Ending is always: "If you liked this video, comment YES, and follow me for more health tips." warm, relaxed, 6 s.
