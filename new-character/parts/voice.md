# Part 2 · The voice

"Do you want me to **describe** her voice for you (recommended), or will you **clone** one in ElevenLabs?"
Describe is the default: if they aren't sure, go with describe.

## Describe
Ask what they hear: "How should she sound? Say it any way you like: 'a southern cowgirl, cute, 25'."
Ask at most 3 or 4 clarifying questions, one at a time, only for what's missing:
age it sounds like · accent · tone (warm, sassy, calm…) · pace and energy.
You already know her age and where she's from (part 1): don't ask those again.

Then write the **ElevenLabs voice design prompt** as one copy block (ElevenLabs → Voices → Create a voice
→ Voice design). Shape: `A [age]-year-old [gender] with a [accent] accent. [Tone and texture]. Speaks
[pace], [energy]. [How she talks to people: friendly, like a big sister…]. Clear, close-mic, natural
recording, no background noise.` Two to three sentences, concrete, no names of real people.

Tell them: "Paste it into ElevenLabs, generate, listen to the options, and save the one you like as
<name>." Save a one-line version as the Voice line in `character.md` (accent, age, tone), plus the voice's name in ElevenLabs.

## Clone
"Great. Clone it in ElevenLabs (Voices → Create a voice → Instant voice clone), test it, and save it as
<name>." When they say it's done:
"Your voice is saved. I'll write a short voice line for the video prompts from what I know about her;
your cloned voice is what she'll really sound like." Write it from her age, where she's from and her
vibe, no questions. Save it in `character.md` with "cloned in ElevenLabs as <name>".

## Why the voice line matters
Omni and Seedance need a voice line in every talking prompt so the accent stays the same from clip to
clip. After the video is made, the voice changer swaps in the saved ElevenLabs voice. In videos with
several people, change only the main character's voice.

Then: "Her voice is done. Last part: her character bible. Ready?"
