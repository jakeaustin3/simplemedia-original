When a shot moves from the character's face to an action close-up, use Flow's Frames mode with **both** a Start and an End frame:
- **Start frame:** medium shot, face to camera, mouth open mid-sentence, the prop already in hand.
- **End frame:** the tight close-up of the action, face out of frame.
- **Prompt:** "camera starts on her face, then slowly pushes in… From the very first frame she is already talking while she <action>… keeps talking once her face leaves the frame".

It costs the same as start-only (6 s 720p = 10 credits).

**Why:** On 2026-10-28 this produced Julia Anderson's clip 2 (tomato squash push-in): talking at 0.00 s, a smooth push-in, and the voice carried over the close-up. The user said "That was fucking amazing. Always remember that."

**How to apply:** Make this the default for any face-to-object move in talking-head videos, and copy the original's framing. The full recipe is in the skill's `parts/omni-storyboard.md` and `rules/METHOD.md` #26. Related: [[ai-influencer-speech-sync]]
